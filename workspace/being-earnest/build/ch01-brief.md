## What happens in this chapter
In the city, a young gentleman lives under the name 诚. His friend 阿龙 discovers, from a note, that his real name is 杰克, not 诚. Cornered, 杰克 confesses his double life: in the country he is 杰克, the serious guardian of a young girl, 西西; to escape to the city and have fun he invented a wild younger 'brother' named 诚, and in the city he simply becomes 诚. 阿龙 laughs and admits he plays a similar trick — he invented a sick friend far away whom he must 'visit' whenever he wants to avoid a boring dinner. The two friends agree that everyone needs a way to escape.
